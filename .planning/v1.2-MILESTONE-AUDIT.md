## Critical Gaps

### 1. Snapshot admission validates identity, not substance

`capture_epoch` retains full system-info, WebSocket, and retained documents.
`validate_operator_snapshot_documents` parses and correlates boot sessions,
operator-snapshot revisions, and retained membership. It does not validate the
six sensor truth/status/stamp fields or the runtime-health and checkpoint facts.
The promotion evaluator then grants the coherent-snapshot and runtime-health
rows from generic eligibility.

This leaves EVD-12 and EVD-15 unsatisfied. The underlying OBS and HLT runtime
wiring remains implemented; the gap is the evidence-backed promotion claim.

### 2. Runtime identity is not independently admitted

The Phase 35 supervisor checks the private device-session Boot B result against
the expected source, reference, ELF, and postcondition. The admitted
`runtime-identity.json`, however, is constructed from package-manifest fields,
and the private result is not an inventory role consumed by the evidence
validator.

This leaves SYS-02 and EVD-11 partial and contributes to EVD-15's unsatisfied
promotion claim.

### 3. No-actuation admission is self-attested

The supervisor writes `no_actuation_verified=true`, hashes that artifact, and
sets the corresponding admission Boolean. The validator checks the Boolean and
digest but receives no independent bounded command/effect trace proving
prohibited writes did not occur.

This leaves EVD-14 and EVD-15 unsatisfied.

- The audit does not invalidate the successful hostname durability and

- It does not claim active control, self-test effects, watchdog intervention,

- It does not authorize a new hardware attempt. The identified gaps are
  software admission and evidence-contract defects and should be closed
  hermetically before deciding whether any new device evidence is needed.
- Attempts 1 through 30 remain immutable non-promotion history. Attempt 31
  remains historical evidence, but its snapshot, health, identity, and
  no-actuation promotions cannot close this audit until the admission gaps are
  repaired and re-evaluated under an exact-current-source contract.

then determine whether Attempt 31 can be re-admitted without changing private
evidence bytes or whether exact-current-source hardware evidence is required.
