# Ultra 205: soak 008, live settings changes during a lease

Soak 008 changed the device's saved frequency and core voltage while a Work
Lease was mining. At 200 s of mining it stepped to 400 MHz / 1100 mV, and at
400 s it stepped back to 485 MHz / 1200 mV. The firmware's live owner
(ADR-0037 decision 4) applied both changes without revoking the lease.
Attempt 001 passed, and an independent review agrees with `complete`. Parity
is not promoted.

## The live changes

Each segment runs from 20 s after a change was read back to the next change.
The last segment ends at the last Gate record with the work gate open.

| Segment             | Settings  | HTTP samples | Measured core mV (median, range) | Counter hashrate (median) | Power (median) | Settled after |
| ------------------- | --------- | ------------ | -------------------------------- | ------------------------- | -------------- | ------------- |
| Before the changes  | 485/1200  | 90           | 1,198 (1,173–1,250)              | 431.1 GH/s                | 11.64 W        | —             |
| After change 1      | 400/1100  | 89           | 1,091 (1,078–1,134)              | 356.3 GH/s                | 8.35 W         | 6.0 s         |
| After change 2      | 485/1200  | 91           | 1,199 (1,108–1,239)              | 431.8 GH/s                | 11.85 W        | 2.0 s         |

- **Voltage:** the measured median stepped by −107 mV for a −100 mV change.
  It returned to within 1 mV of the first segment. The judge allows ±30 mV
  on each step.
- **Frequency:** the median counter hashrate fell to 0.827 of the first
  segment, against 400/485 = 0.825. It came back to 1.002. The BM1366 hash
  counters measure work done, so this is direct evidence that the frequency
  changed. The judge allows ±0.12.
- **Order:** voltage settled 6.0 s after the step down. The owner lowers
  frequency and nonce space before voltage, so voltage moves last. Going up,
  voltage moves first, and it settled in 2.0 s.
- **Thermals:** at 400/1100 the chip ran 53–56 C at 8.15–8.87 W, with the fan
  at 2,442–2,988 RPM.
- **Settings:** the saved settings matched the target on every mining HTTP
  sample in every segment. Ending on the starting pair kept the preservation
  fingerprint equal to the page's baseline.
- **Outliers:** seven single readings were more than 40 mV from their
  segment's median, for example 1,108 mV at about 596 s, between neighbours
  of 1,188 and 1,198 mV at a normal 12.06 W. None moved a median. They look
  like momentary ADC misreads.

## Gaps

The largest gaps overlapped, about 16–20 s after the second change was
requested: 4,052 ms on HTTP and 3,098 ms on the WebSocket. The voltage had
already settled about 14 s earlier. The evidence points to network delivery,
not a device stall:
- The device's worst send times stayed at 23 ms (HTTP) and 17 ms
  (WebSocket), with no failures.
- The WebSocket revisions it produced during the gap arrived in a 70 ms
  burst.
- Wi-Fi RSSI dipped from −34 to −50 dBm at that moment.
- The Gate's USB records kept their roughly 1 s cadence, with active time
  advancing and no revocation.

Both gaps are within the judge's bounds, and soak 002 had larger ones
(3,551 ms WebSocket, 4,632 ms HTTP). The next-largest gaps were 2.26 s on
HTTP and 974 ms on the WebSocket.

## Non-claims

- This covers live changes between 485/1200 and 400/1100, the two pairs that
  already had hardware evidence. Other pairs inside the catalog range remain
  without evidence.
- The catalog-maximum (575 MHz / 1300 mV) shutdown tail remains open under
  `task-device-config-profile-evidence-soaks`.
- HTTP was the only writer. BAP `frequency` and `asic_voltage` reach the same
  settings owner (`task-parity-bap-settings-effects`), but BAP was not
  exercised here.
- Parity rows are not promoted by this run.
