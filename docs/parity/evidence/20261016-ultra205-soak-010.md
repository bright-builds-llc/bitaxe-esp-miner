# Ultra 205: soak 010, BAP settings during a lease

Soak 010 changed the device's settings through the Bitaxe Accessory Port (BAP)
while a Work Lease was mining. It used the repo's `bap-client` over the
owner's CP2102 adapter on J6 (GND, RXD and TXD only), as an accessory would.
- Every `SET` was acknowledged or read back exactly as the reference does.
- The device applied each setting live: frequency, core voltage and the fan
  mode.

Attempt 001 passed, and an independent review agrees with `complete`. Parity
is not promoted.

## The BAP operations

| Active time | Frames sent, with the device's answers                                                                                       |
| ----------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 100,068 ms  | `SET show_new_block 1` → `ACK 1`; `REQ` → `1`; `SET show_new_block 0` → `ACK 0`; `REQ` → `0`; `SET found_block 0` → `ACK 0`; `REQ found_block` → `block_found 0` |
| 180,068 ms  | `SET frequency 400` → `ACK 400.00`; `SET asic_voltage 1100` → `ACK 1100`                                                       |
| 280,069 ms  | `SET fan_speed 80`: no answer and no refusal within 45 s, as the reference sends no acknowledgement                            |
| 420,069 ms  | `SET asic_voltage 1200` → `ACK 1200`; `SET frequency 485` → `ACK 485.00`; `SET fan_speed 100` (silent); `SET auto_fan 1` → `ACK auto_fan_speed_set` |

The order kept every saved pair to one with hardware evidence: frequency went
down before voltage and voltage up before frequency. Wi-Fi `ssid` and
`password` were not sent, because they restart the device and carry
credentials.

## Effects, from the HTTP journal

Each segment runs from 20 s after a change's last answer to the next change.
The last segment ends at the last Gate record with the work gate open.

| Segment          | Saved settings            | Samples | Measured core mV (median, range) | Hashrate (median) | Power  | Fan RPM (median) |
| ---------------- | ------------------------- | ------- | -------------------------------- | ----------------- | ------ | ---------------- |
| Before           | 485/1200, auto fan        | 79      | 1,199 (1,143–1,236)              | 434.1 GH/s        | 11.57 W | 2,773           |
| After 180 s      | 400/1100, auto fan        | 39      | 1,097 (1,073–1,147)              | 367.0 GH/s        | 8.35 W  | 2,602           |
| After 280 s      | 400/1100, manual fan 80%  | 37      | 1,085 (1,004–1,129)              | 356.4 GH/s        | 8.40 W  | 7,468           |
| After 420 s      | 485/1200, auto fan        | 56      | 1,198 (1,183–1,234)              | 421.1 GH/s        | 11.67 W | 2,942           |

- **Settings:** every mining HTTP sample in every segment carried the saved
  frequency, voltage and fan settings.
- **Voltage:** the measured median stepped −102 and −114 mV for −100, and
  came back within 1 mV.
- **Frequency:** the counter hashrate fell to 0.845 and 0.821 of the first
  segment (400/485 = 0.825), and came back to 0.97.
- **Fan:** under the manual setting the fan ran 2.87 times faster than under
  the automatic fan just before. The chip cooled from 53–56 C to 44–46 C.
  After `auto_fan 1` the automatic controller took over again.

## Observations

- **Manual fan speed:** at 80% duty the fan read 7,387–7,468 RPM. At 100%,
  in the last change before `auto_fan 1`, it read 7,346–7,427 RPM.
  - The firmware sets the duty as asked, with the same EMC2101 code as the
    reference: (63 × 80) / 100 = 50 (`fan_controller_plan.rs`,
    `safety_adapter/emc2101.rs`; `fan_controller_task.c`).
  - So this fan reaches full speed at or below about 79% duty. The small
    difference follows the input voltage.
  - The samples record no duty field, so the applied duty itself is not
    observed.
- **Voltage under the manual fan:** the median read 12 mV lower than in the
  automatic-fan segment at the same settings, while the chip ran about 10 C
  cooler. One low reading of 1,004 mV did not move the median.
- **Restore not needed:** the plan ended on the starting profile, so the
  settings fingerprint matched the page's baseline. No HTTP restore was sent.

## Non-claims

- Wi-Fi `ssid` and `password` were not exercised. Their restart path is
  covered by unit tests only.
- The BAP subscriptions of settings were not exercised; the reference has
  none for frequency, voltage or fan mode.
- Fan speed against duty (how linear the fan is) is not measured.
- Parity rows are not promoted by this run.
