# Gamma 601: the 4-hour soak at the default profile

Phase F4.2 of the Gamma 601 bring-up (`task-gamma-601-bring-up`, ADR-0041)
ran the test-only `gamma601-mining` firmware through the local-mining soak
`gamma601-soak-001`: 4 hours of mining accumulated at upstream's default
525 MHz / 1150 mV with the owner's pool, within a 5-hour deadline, outages
the device recovers from within 60 s tolerated (the rule of the 205's soak
006), then a paused, stopped device and production reinstalled. Attempt
001 passed.

- **Commands** (the contract in `TASKS.md`): as phase F4.1's, with the soak
  `gamma601-soak-001` and `run` detached under `nohup caffeinate -is`.
- **Sources:** the attempt at `e1fa5a47`; the reference at `c1915b0`.
- **Manifests,** retained privately with the captures: the run's
  `firmware_variant=gamma601-mining`, board 601, source `e1fa5a47`; the
  reinstall's `firmware_variant=production`, the same source, clean.
- **Pool:** `pool_config: local-owner-supplied`.
- **Tab visibility:** before the handoff, the dedicated Gate tab read
  visible throughout 540 s with no browser call.

## The soak's verdict

`result=passed`, no failures:

| Metric | Value | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mining accumulated | 14,401,349 ms (4 h 0 min) over 6,902 active samples | 4 h within 5 h |
| Shares accepted / rejected | 440 / 0 | at least one accepted |
| Hashrate | mean 1068 GH/s; 5th to 95th percentile 949 to 1194 (525 MHz × 2040 small cores ≈ 1071 GH/s) | nonzero |
| Power peak | 20.9 W | under 22 W |
| Chip temperature peak | 71.5 C | under 75 C |
| Regulator temperature peak | 80.0 C | under 90 C |
| Input voltage | 5094 to 5195 mV | 4500 to 5500 mV |
| Fan | at least 3446 rpm (at the start) | nonzero |
| Wi-Fi | at least -52 dBm | |
| Outages | one, 55.7 s | each under 60 s |

- **Steady state:** in the last hour the power read 20.1 to 20.5 W, the
  chip 67.9 to 69.3 C and the regulator 75.8 to 77.3 C, with the
  automatic fan at its top, about 8,000 rpm, and a mean of 1067 GH/s.
  Minutes 25 to 85 read a little wider (20.0 to 20.5 W, chip 67.5 to
  69.9 C, regulator 75.0 to 78.3 C). Through the middle hours the chip
  read 70 C or more in 1,078 samples, peaking at 71.5 C.
- **The outage:** about 20 minutes in (device uptime 1591 s), the Wi-Fi
  link dropped (`wifi_reconnect=disconnected reason=beacon_timeout`) and
  reconnected on its first retry about 9.5 s later. HTTP went unanswered
  for 20.4 s. The local run then ended (`ending=Some(Requested)`, uptime
  1622 s) and stopped through its ordered safe stop (`stopping` samples at
  5.0 W). A new run began at once (`local_mining=run_began generation=3`),
  ramped up through `safe_blocked` (5.7 rising to 17.4 W) and was hashing
  again about 14 s after the run ended, with no reset and no command. The
  soak measures the outage from the last hashing sample before the gap to
  the next: 55.7 s, under its 60 s tolerance. The break log journaled
  these lines.
- **The stop:** the soak paused the device, and it read paused and stopped
  31 s later at 5.0 W, with start-on-boot off. One boot session held
  through the run.

## Production afterwards

The receive-only capture after the reinstall holds 56 safe-boot records:
`built=601 stored=601 identity=match`, `reset_held=true`, `off=confirmed`,
one boot. The USB profile reads `worker_started` with a confirmed
baseline. The boot read `boot_ordinal=1`, as the earlier production boots
did; recorded, not investigated.

## Effects

One mining install, the handoff, start-on-boot saved off, the pool and the
525/1150 profile saved, 4 hours of local mining, the device's own stop and
restart across the Wi-Fi outage, the soak's pause and stop, then one
production install and its receive-only capture. There was no Worker
Start or lease, factory reset, other frequency or voltage, network
discovery beyond the handoff, direct UART or pin access.
