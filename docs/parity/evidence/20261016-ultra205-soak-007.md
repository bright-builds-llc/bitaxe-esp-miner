# Ultra 205: soak 007, the first soak on device settings

Soak 007 is the first soak under ADR-0037. The Work Lease carries no hardware
profile. The device's own settings chose the profile: 485 MHz, 1200 mV and the
automatic fan. Attempt 002 passed, and an independent review agrees with
`complete`. Parity is not promoted.

Attempt 001 mined the full budget but sealed `unverified` because of a fault
in the soak tooling's ordering (see below). Attempt 002 ran at the fix commit.

## Mining time and shutdown (ADR-0037 decision 5)

- The work gate closed at exactly 600,000 active ms. The ledger charged
  600,000 ms, the mining time alone.
- The ordered shutdown then ran outside the budget. Generation elapsed time
  was 617,621 ms: about 9,957 ms of preparation, 600,000 ms of mining and
  about 7,664 ms from revocation to the end of the pre-reset shutdown. That
  is well inside the 19,050 ms closed tail for 485 MHz, and the 22,550 ms
  `shutdown_budget_ms` the device reports.
- Including cooling proof, the Gate saw revocation to `fan_paused` in about
  23 s.

The automatic controller held the chip at about 60 C, its `temptarget`, at

Preparation's 100% fan and cooling proof are unchanged (ADR-0025): the fan read
4,289 RPM at the restored baseline.

## Attempt 001: a tooling-order fault

Attempt 001 (package `2f65509c`, soak ordinal 1) passed every device
criterion:
- 600,000 ms of mining, ended by the budget, with a shutdown of about 7.7 s;
- 26 accepted and 0 rejected shares;
- 20/20 windows credited;
- samples within 5.23–5.27 V, 10.38–12.51 W, 35–61 C and 2,433–3,938 RPM.

It sealed `unverified` with `cleanup_unconfirmed`. The Gate page keeps the first
settings fingerprint it observes as its preservation baseline. The soak wrote
the device settings from that same page, after that first observation, so the
final comparison reported `settings_match: false`. Soaks 002–006 wrote no
settings.

The fix (`6b3e567a`):
- `serve` refuses the budget review until a page reloaded after the write
  reports a fresh baseline with `settings_match: true`.
- The issuance records that baseline, and the judge fails a final state from
  any other page.

In attempt 002 the WebSocket observer kept one unbroken connection across the
page reload, so the idle proof still passed.

## Non-claims

- This is evidence for 485 MHz / 1200 mV with the automatic fan only. The
  live frequency change and the catalog-maximum (575 MHz / 1300 mV) shutdown
  tail remain open under `task-device-config-profile-evidence-soaks`.
- Parity rows are not promoted by this run.
