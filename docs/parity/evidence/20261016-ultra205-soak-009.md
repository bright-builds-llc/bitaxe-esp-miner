# Ultra 205: soak 009, the catalog-maximum frequency

Soak 009 ran the Ultra 205 catalog's maximum frequency, 575 MHz, at 1200 mV
with the automatic fan, chosen by the device's own settings (ADR-0037). It
exercises the longest closed shutdown tail, bounded at 22,550 ms. The owner
chose 1200 mV on 2026-10-16: 575 MHz at 1300 mV is estimated at about 16 W,
over the 15 W live stop limit. Attempt 001 passed, and an independent review
agrees with `complete`. Parity is not promoted.

## The 575 MHz profile

| Measure                   | Soak 007 (485/1200)  | Soak 009 (575/1200)  |
| ------------------------- | -------------------- | -------------------- |
| Work-gate power           | 10.36–12.42 W        | 12.24–14.34 W        |
| Median power              | about 11.7 W         | 13.75 W              |
| Chip temperature          | 35–61 C              | 37–61 C              |
| Fan                       | 2,433–3,950 RPM      | 2,420–5,775 RPM      |
| Counter hashrate (median) | 431.1 GH/s (soak 008)| 508.4 GH/s           |
| Shutdown on the device    | about 7.7 s          | about 9.3 s          |

- **Power:** the highest sample was 14.34 W, 0.66 W under the 15 W stop
  limit. The median matched the estimate made before the run (about 14 W).
- **Frequency:** the median counter hashrate was 1.179 times soak 008's
  485 MHz segment, against 575/485 = 1.186. Both medians use the same
  segment rule: from 20 s after mining starts until the work gate closes.
  `miningActive` stays true through the shutdown with a stale hashrate, so
  those samples are excluded.
- **Fan:** the automatic fan held the chip at about 60 C, its target. It did
  that at a median of about 5,360 RPM, against about 3,640 at 485 MHz.
- **Shutdown:** the shutdown took about 1.6 s longer than at 485 MHz. That fits
  a ramp down from a higher frequency. The closed tail has 84 ramp steps at
  575 MHz and 70 at 485 MHz, a ratio of 1.20. The measured ratio is 1.21,
  about 110 ms per step against the 250 ms the bound allows. Preparation took
  about 11.8 s, against about 10 s at 485 MHz, for the same reason on the way
  up.
- **Restore:** after completion, while the device was idle, `serve` wrote
  485 MHz, 1200 mV and the automatic fan back and read them back. Later
  leases therefore do not inherit 575 MHz. The final state had been taken
  before the restore, so preservation compared the soak's own settings.

## Non-claims

- This covers 575 MHz at 1200 mV only. The 1300 mV catalog corner was not
  run, because it is estimated to exceed the 15 W limit.
- The 14.34 W peak leaves little margin. Warmer ambient air or another board
  could trip the limit at this profile.
- Parity rows are not promoted by this run.
