committed SYS-004 exact-package result. It contains the exact 11 required slot
files, uses current evidence dispositions, and keeps private detector, device,
network, serial, API, WebSocket, and credential material outside the repository.

typed operator workflow on board 205. Mining, shares, production safe stop,
credentials during mining, hardware-control effects, recovery, and other boards
